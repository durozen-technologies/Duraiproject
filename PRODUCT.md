# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Shop staff and owners at Durai poultry trading who manage parties, day bills, collections, drivers, and expenses—often on a phone during busy market hours, moving quickly between list and entry tasks.

## Product Purpose

A mobile trading ledger for poultry purchase and sale operations: record parties and balances, day bills, payments, drivers, expenses, and reports. Success means fast, accurate entry with clear To Pay / To Receive balances and reliable history.

## Brand Personality

Practical, trustworthy, decisive. Feels like a working counter tool—clear and efficient, not decorative.

## Anti-references

Generic purple SaaS dashboards; cream/editorial magazine layouts; glassmorphism; neon dark-mode fintech; over-decorated card grids; bottom-sheet forms that feel like half-finished pages when a focused dialog would do.

## Design Principles

- Task first: every screen should get the user through one job with minimal friction.
- Familiar product affordances: standard lists, filters, and dialogs over invented patterns.
- Semantic money language: To Pay (CR) and To Receive (DR) stay consistent everywhere.
- Hierarchy through weight and space, not decoration.
- Errors explain how to fix the field, not just that something failed.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for body and controls. Touch targets at least 44px. Respect reduced motion where animations are added. Form errors must be visible as text, not color alone.
