# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Poultry farm owners and managers who need to enter data quickly and see analytics clearly. They value efficiency, reliability, and clear contrast.

## Product Purpose
LedgerDesk serves as the operational dashboard for farm management, tracking stock, sales, expenses, and parties.

## Brand Personality
Professional, agricultural, trustworthy, and modern. It relies on a strong, dependable dark green (`#006948`) as its core identity.

## Anti-references
- No generic, ultra-rounded "SaaS" aesthetics with low-contrast gray text on off-white.
- No playful consumer app elements.
- No cryptic crypto-dashboard styling.

## Design Principles
- **Data over decoration**: Layouts should serve the content, not hide it behind glassmorphism or unnecessary borders.
- **Clear contrast**: Readability is paramount for users who may be outside or in bright environments.
- **Tactile efficiency**: Interactions (focus states, buttons) should feel instantaneous and distinctly tactile.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High contrast requirements for readability in varying lighting conditions.
- Clear error states and large enough tap targets for fast navigation.
